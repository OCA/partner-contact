Usage
=====

After installing the module, you'll see a new "Owner Company" field on
contact forms:

When selecting a contact in a dropdown selection field, only the
contacts owned by the current company, or global, are proposed. Contacts
owned by other companies can still be selected, by extact match of name,
reference code, email, or Tax/VAt number.
