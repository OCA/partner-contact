This addon allows you to assign profit ranges to contacts, making it
easier to categorize and segment them based on their profitability. This
way, businesses can easily identify and manage their most valuable
clients.

Usage
=====

To use this module, you need to:

- Go to any partner's form.
- You will find the new fields after *Tags* field.

To manage revenue ranges, you need to:

- Go to *Contacts > Configuration > Revenue ranges*.
