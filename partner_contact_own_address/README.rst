In Odoo, a contact of type "Contact" shares the address of its parent
company. Editing the address on the contact changes the company address,
and editing the company address changes the address of all its contacts.

This module adds an "Own Address" option on contacts. A contact with
this option keeps its own address, for example a person working from
home, while it stays a contact of the company:

- Editing the contact address does not change the company address.
- Editing the company address does not change the contact address.
- Moving the contact to another company keeps its address.

Usage
=====

On a contact of a company, tick "Own Address" and enter the contact
address. In the contact popup of the company form, tick "Own Address" to
show the address fields.

Untick "Own Address" to go back to the company address: the contact
address is replaced by the company address.

To turn an existing address (e.g. an "Other Address") into a contact
with its own address, open it from the company's Contacts tab, choose
"Contact" and tick "Own Address" before saving. Saving the type change
alone replaces its address with the company address.
