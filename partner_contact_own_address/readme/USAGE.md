On a contact of a company, tick "Own Address" and enter the contact address.
In the contact popup of the company form, tick "Own Address" to show the
address fields.

Untick "Own Address" to go back to the company address: the contact address is
replaced by the company address.

To turn an existing address (e.g. an "Other Address") into a contact with its
own address, open it from the company's Contacts tab, choose "Contact" and tick
"Own Address" before saving. Saving the type change alone replaces its address
with the company address.
