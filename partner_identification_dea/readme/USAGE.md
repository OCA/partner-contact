On the partner form you will see DEA, medical license, and controlled
substance fields computed from the **ID Numbers** tab.

1. Open a contact.
2. Go to the **ID Numbers** tab.
3. Add a number with category **DEA Number**, **Medical License**, or
   **Controlled Substance**.
4. Set status to **Running** and fill **Valid until**.
5. The matching stored fields on the contact update automatically.
6. On a quotation, you can pick a **DEA license** for the customer.
7. A daily scheduled action emails contacts whose DEA expires in 30 days.
