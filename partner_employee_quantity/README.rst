This module allows to set employee quantity for partners, and categorize
them by ranges.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Configuration
=============

To manage available ranges, you need to:

1. Go to *Contacts > Configuration > Ranges by quantity of employees*.
2. Add or edit any range naming it.

Usage
=====

To record how many employees a company has:

1. Go to *Contacts* and open a company.

   In Odoo 20.0 a contact is considered a company when it has a *Tax ID*
   and is not a contact of another company. Individuals and the contacts
   of a company do not show the employee fields.

2. Open the *Notes* tab. In the *Employee quantity* section, fill in:

   - *Employee Quantity*: the number of employees.
   - *Employee quantity range*: the range this company belongs to (for
     example *11-50 Employees*). Ranges are defined in *Contacts >
     Configuration > Ranges by quantity of employees*.

To see your companies by size:

1. Go to *Contacts* and open the list view.
2. In the search box, open the filter menu and choose *Group By >
   Employee Quantity*.
